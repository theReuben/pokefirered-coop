Headline fix · diagnosis

Co-op battle party corruption FIXED

A gap flagged on 06-17 turned out to be worse than recorded — three independent ways a boss double battle could permanently damage your party.

① Destructive reorder

CB2_CoopPartySelected reorders gPlayerParty[0..n] in place. With more than 3 mons, selecting a subset permanently overwrote unselected lead mons.

＋

② Early partner write

HandleRemotePartySync wrote the partner's mons straight into gPlayerParty[3..5] — possibly before you even opened your own menu, clobbering slots 3–5 of a big party pre-stash.

＋

③ No restore

After the battle, nothing put anything back. Partner mons stayed in your party; your evicted mons were gone.

The two scripts run unsynchronized, so the corruption window existed on every co-op boss battle with a party of 4+ — it just hadn't been hit in fixed-fixture testing.
Headline fix · mechanism

The fix: stash → side-buffer → restore

Mirrors how vanilla handles INGAME_PARTNER battles (CB2_EndDebugBattle) — three parts, one lifecycle.

Stash before menu

ScrCmd_waitcoopparty calls SavePlayerParty() before the selection menu opens; selected slot indices are recorded in coopSelectedSlots[].

→

Partner stays outside

Partner party decodes into an EWRAM side buffer (sPartnerBattleParty) and only enters gPlayerParty inside Multiplayer_SetupCoopBattle — never earlier.

→

Restore after battle

Multiplayer_OnBattleEnd writes each participant's post-battle exp/HP/status back to its original stash slot, then LoadPlayerParty(). Runs regardless of connState — the disconnect/grace path restores too.

  • Non-participants survive untouched; partner mons are evicted cleanly.
  • New native tests: TestRemotePartySyncStagesOutsidePlayerParty, TestCoopBattleEndRestoresParty.
  • Full suite green: 1210 + 49 + 39 + 236 + 307 assertions, 0 failures; ROM builds clean.

Field-trainer lock released at the right layer

Follow-up to the #18a beacon work: the lock now releases in Multiplayer_OnBattleEnd, not the per-frame Update poll.

  • Why it matters: releasing from the Update poll raced the battle-end sequence — the beacon could re-carry a stale busy state after the battle was already over.
  • Now: lock release is an explicit battle-lifecycle event, in the same hook that restores the party — one place where "the battle is over" is true.
  • Same commit added navigation tools for the harness: collision-map rendering and A*-driven path_to / drive_to_tile, so test agents stop guessing tile counts.

Discovered · not yet fixed

The starter_denied message goes nowhere OPEN

A spec-vs-implementation gap that only bites on the real relay — invisible to all MCP testing.

Relay ✓

PartyKit server implements and tests starter_denied: on simultaneous same-species picks, first pick wins and the loser is told.

→

Bridge ✗

serial_bridge.rs maps the message to None — it is silently dropped on the floor.

→

ROM ✗

No packet type, no handler. The slower player believes their pick succeeded → permanent starter desync between the two saves.

  • Why tests missed it: the in-process MCP relay doesn't arbitrate picks, so the losing path never executes locally.
  • Fix directions on the table: (a) new relay→ROM packet across all 5 protocol layers whose handler reverts the pick and re-locks the ball, or (b) drop relay arbitration entirely — carry starter state on the beacon with a deterministic host-wins tiebreak.
